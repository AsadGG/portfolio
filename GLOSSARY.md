# Portfolio

A personal portfolio site that presents its owner and the work they have done, organised by where that work happened.

## Language

### Identity

**Profile**:
The single record describing the portfolio owner: name, Headline, Intro and contact details.
_Avoid_: User, about, bio

**Headline**:
The owner's current professional title, shown under the name. It is the only job title on the site.
_Avoid_: Title, role, position

**Intro**:
The short self-introduction that opens the site.
_Avoid_: Bio, about me, summary

**Highlight**:
A standalone career achievement shown in the Highlights section, not tied to a single Project.
_Avoid_: Achievement, key achievement, impact

### Skills

**Skill**:
A named technology or competency the owner has worked with.
_Avoid_: Tech, tool, stack item

**Unlisted Skill**:
A Skill that appears only on the Projects that used it and is left out of the skills section.
_Avoid_: Hidden skill, minor skill

**Skill Category**:
A named grouping of Skills (e.g. Languages, Frontend) used to organise the skills section.
_Avoid_: Skill group, section

### Experience

**Engagement**:
A period of time during which the owner did work for others. Every Engagement is either an Employment or Freelance.
_Avoid_: Job, experience entry, position

**Employment**:
An Engagement at a single Company. Carries no job title of its own.
_Avoid_: Job, role

**Company**:
The organisation an Employment was with.
_Avoid_: Employer, organisation

**Freelance**:
The single Engagement grouping all independent work.
_Avoid_: Freelance work, contracting, gigs

**Client**:
The party a Freelance Project was done for. Optional; may be withheld.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Present**:
The state of an Engagement that has no end date, i.e. ongoing.
_Avoid_: Current, now, ongoing

**Project**:
A piece of work belonging to exactly one Engagement.
_Avoid_: Work item, case study

**Part**:
A distinct piece of a Project (e.g. its API, admin console or mobile app) with its own Summary, Description, Images and Skills. A Project has zero or more Parts.
_Avoid_: Component, module, deliverable, sub-project

**Summary**:
A one-to-three-sentence overview of an Engagement, Project or Part, shown before any detail.
_Avoid_: Tagline, excerpt, blurb

**Description**:
The full account of a Project or Part.
_Avoid_: Details, body

**Image**:
A visual of a Project or Part, always with alt text. Either may have none (e.g. under NDA).
_Avoid_: Screenshot, media, asset

### Education

**Education**:
The single university record of the owner's degree.
_Avoid_: University, academics, qualification
